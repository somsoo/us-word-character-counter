🧮 How the counts work

  • Words: groups of characters between spaces or line breaks that contain at least one letter or number. "well-known", "don't", "3.5" and "U.S." are one word each; a lone dash or emoji is not a word.
  • Characters: every character as you see it, including spaces and tabs but not line breaks. Emoji and accented letters count once, even when a computer stores them as several code points.
  • Characters without spaces: the same, minus spaces, tabs and line breaks.
  • Sentences: text that ends at . ! ? or … followed by a space or line break, plus the end of each paragraph. Decimal numbers such as 3.5 do not end a sentence, but abbreviations such as Mr. can.
  • Paragraphs: each line that has text on it. Blank lines are skipped.
  • Reading and speaking time: words ÷ words per minute, rounded to the nearest second.
  • Averages: letters and numbers per word (punctuation left out), and words per sentence, both to one decimal.

Reading speed source: Brysbaert, M. (2019). How many words do we read per minute? A review and meta-analysis of reading rate. Journal of Memory and Language, 109. The review of 190 studies found average silent reading rates of 238 words per minute for English non-fiction and 260 for fiction. The speaking default of 150 words per minute is a common pace for speeches and presentations.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

A word is any group of characters between spaces or line breaks that contains at least one letter or number. So well-known, don't, 3.5 and U.S. each count as one word, and a dash on its own counts as none. This is close to how most word processors count.

Do characters include spaces?

Characters with spaces counts every letter, number, symbol, emoji, space and tab, but not line breaks. Characters without spaces leaves out all spaces, tabs and line breaks. An emoji or a letter with an accent counts as one character, the way you see it on screen.

How is reading time estimated?

Reading time is the word count divided by a reading speed. The default, 238 words per minute, is the average silent reading rate for adults reading English non-fiction in a 2019 review of 190 studies by Marc Brysbaert in the Journal of Memory and Language; the same review found 260 words per minute for fiction. You can enter your own speed.

How is speaking time estimated?

Speaking time uses 150 words per minute by default, a typical pace for a speech or presentation. Enter a lower speed for a slow, formal talk or a higher one for a quick, casual one.

How are sentences and paragraphs counted?

A sentence ends at a period, question mark, exclamation mark or ellipsis followed by a space or line break, and at the end of each paragraph. Abbreviations such as Mr. or e.g. can add an extra sentence. Each line that has text on it counts as one paragraph; blank lines are skipped.

⚠️ Please note

Word processors, schools, publishers and social apps each count a little differently, especially for hyphens, links, emoji and abbreviations. When a limit really matters, check the count in the tool or form that enforces it.
